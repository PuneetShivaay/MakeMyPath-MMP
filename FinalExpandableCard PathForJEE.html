About Exam

+
  • JEE (Main) is a Computer Based Test (CBT) conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for the students want to pursue undergraduate courses (B.E./B.Tech.) in top engineering and technogy institutes (NITs, IIITs, GFTIs etc.) in India. It is the first phase of JEE (Advanced) Examination. The JEE (Main) examination is conducted in two sessions and candidates can take this either in the month of January or April. Candidates can appear for one or both the sessions of January and April. The better of these two NTA scores will be considered for rank list.

Eligiblity & Age Limit

+
  • Qualifying / appearing Exam Class 12th Examination / Equivalent Qualifying Examination
    Year of passing Exam 2019, 2020 or 2021 for appearing in JEE (Main)-2021
    Required Subjects in Qualifying/Appearing Exam Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry / Biology / Biotechnology / Technical Vocational Subject, Language and Optional Subject.
    Qualifying Marks 75% aggregate (65% aggregate in case of SC/ST/PWD) or top 20 percentile of the respective board in order to avail admission into the NITs, IIITs and GFTIs.
  • Age Limit :- There is no age limit for the candidates for appearing in the JEE (Main) examination. The candidates who have passed class 12th/equivalent examination in 2019, 2020 or appearing in 2021 irrespective of their age can appear in JEE (Main) examination-2021. The candidates should also satisfy themselves about their fulfilling the age limit requirements of the Institutes in which they are desirous of taking admission.

Paper Pattern

+
  • Mode of exam :- Computer Based Test (CBT) Online
  • Language Options :- English, Hindi and Gujarati
  • Total no. of Questions:- 75
  • Exam Duration :- 3 Hours
  • Subject wise distribution of Questions and Marks
    Subject No. of Questions Marks
    Mathematics

    		 100
    Physics 20+5*
    		 100
    Chemistry
    		 20+5*
    		 100
    Total
    		 75 300
    *20 questions will be Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and 5 questions will be Numerical Value Questions (NVQs).
  • Marking Scheme :-
    Correct Answer
    		 Four mark (+4)
    Incorrect Answer
    		 Minus one mark (-1)
    Unattempted Answer / Marked for Review
    		 No mark (0)
    Incorrect Answer for Numerical Value Question
    		 No mark (0)
