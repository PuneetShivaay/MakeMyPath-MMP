FTII

About Exam

+
  • The Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) is an autonomous civil service training institute for Indian Information Service cadre under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting of the Government of India and aided by the Central Government of India. The institute provides PG Diploma and Certificate courses in various disciplines of film and television.
